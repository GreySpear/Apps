🧺 Ingredients

4 servings
2 chicken breasts
1 tbsp olive oil
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 cup heavy cream
½ cup sun-dried tomatoes
2 cups baby spinach
½ cup parmesan, grated

👩‍🍳 Steps

1
Season chicken with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high.
2
Sear chicken 5–6 min per side until golden and cooked through, then set aside.
3
Add garlic and sun-dried tomatoes; cook 1 min until fragrant.
4
Pour in cream, simmer gently, then stir in parmesan until smooth.
